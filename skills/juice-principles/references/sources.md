# Sources: what each book contributes

Back to [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). Web sources with dates: [RESEARCH.md](../RESEARCH.md).

The knowledge base summarizes these books in its own words and cites them by chapter and page. No text from the books ships with the plugin. Read 2026-10-26.

| Key | Book | Edition used | Citation style | Contributes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Swink | Steve Swink, *Game Feel: A Game Designer's Guide to Virtual Sensation*, Morgan Kaufmann, 2009 | 1st | chapter, printed page | The model: real-time control + simulated space + polish. Perception thresholds (~100 ms fusion, ~240 ms correction cycle, 50-200/30-100/25-170 ms processor ranges), ADSR response envelopes, polish as effects → groups → inferred physical properties, "polish cannot fix control". P-01, P-02, P-03, P-05, P-06, P-07, P-14, P-16, P-20, P-22. |
| Schell | Jesse Schell, *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses*, 2nd ed., CRC Press, 2015 | 2nd | chapter, lens number, printed page | The lens format the plugin's questions follow, plus the source lenses: #2 Essential Experience, #17 Toy, #54 Accessibility, #59 Control, #61-#67 (interface, transparency, feedback, juiciness, primality, channels, modes), #71 Beauty. Second-order motion; "sound maps to touch". P-08, P-09, P-10, P-21; lenses J1, J5-J7, J10, J12. |
| Hodent | Celia Hodent, *The Gamer's Brain: How Neuroscience and UX Can Impact Video Game Design*, CRC Press, 2017 | 1st (EPUB, no page numbers) | chapter, section | Why feedback works and when it becomes noise: constructed perception, limited attention, inattentional and change blindness, cognitive load, signal detection, signs and feedback, consistency, form follows function, accessibility. Places game feel in "engage-ability" (emotion) and the 3Cs. P-12, P-13, P-15, P-18, P-19; lens J11. |
| Rogers 2e | Scott Rogers, *Level Up! The Guide to Great Video Game Design*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2014 | 2nd (PDF with text layer: 475 of 500 pages had text, so no OCR was needed) | chapter, PDF page | Practical checks: camera (visibility, collision, shake as a measured consequence, motion sickness), controls (immediate acknowledgement, ergonomics, remapping), jump readability, hit confirmation, hit pause, proportional hit reactions, HUD placement and the HUD not being the only channel, layered audio as gameplay information. P-04, P-10, P-11, P-17. |
| Rogers 3e | Scott Rogers, *Level Up!*, 3rd ed., Wiley, 2023 (EPUB) | 3rd | chapter, section | What changed: VR in the camera chapter (motion comfort), an explicit accessibility and alternate-controls checklist (feedback toggles, colour-blind options, reversed sticks), an expanded combat-feedback section. P-18. |

## Citing in reports

Use the principle ID, not the book: "(P-04 hit-stop)". The ID resolves here and to principles.md. Name the book only when the user asks why.
